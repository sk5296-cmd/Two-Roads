Two Epics

The Core Parallel: The American Revolution (detailed in McCullough's 1776) and the narrative of Yetzias Mitzrayim (the Exodus) in the Torah both center on an oppressed populace successfully defying an established, dominant empire to forge a sovereign identity.

The Crossings

Hover to reveal the pivotal moments.

Defying the Elements: Bnei Yisrael's miraculous parting of the Yam Suph mirrors Washington's daring, icy crossing of the Delaware River. Both were desperate, pivotal maneuvers requiring immense faith and resolute leadership against insurmountable odds.
