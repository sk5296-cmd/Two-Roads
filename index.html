Project Introduction

Two Epic Narratives

This project explores the striking parallels between two of history's most famous stories of liberation: the American Revolution, as detailed in David McCullough's 1776, and the biblical Exodus from Egypt. While separated by thousands of years, both historical events share a common theme of an oppressed people fighting against a massive empire to secure their freedom.

Crossing the Waters

At the center of both stories is a dramatic crossing that changed everything. The Israelites famously crossed the Red Sea to escape the pharaoh's army, while George Washington's army crossed the icy Delaware River in a freezing storm to save the American cause. Both critical moments required immense faith, courage, and leadership against impossible odds.
